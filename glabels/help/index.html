Using this guide

  • Type in the search box on the left ([[Ctrl+K]]) to search every page. Use the arrow keys and [[Enter]] to open a result; the words you searched for are highlighted on the page.
  • [[Ctrl+F]] finds text on the current page; [[F3]] and [[Shift+F3]] jump between matches.
  • [[Alt+Left]] and [[Alt+Right]] go back and forward. [[Ctrl++]] and [[Ctrl+-]] change the text size.
  • This window is separate from your label, so you can keep it open beside your work.
