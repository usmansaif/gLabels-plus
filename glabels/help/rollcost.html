Ribbon and label cost estimator

Work out how many label rolls and ink ribbons a thermal printing job needs and what they cost. Open it from Tools > Ribbon & Label Cost Estimator.... It opens in its own window, so you can keep it open while you work.

1. Currency and units

Choose the Currency of your prices. It is only shown in front of amounts; nothing is converted. If yours is not in the list, choose Other... and type a symbol such as Rs. Choose millimeters, centimeters or inches for the label sizes.

2. Labels

Click Choose... to pick a product from the library. Its label size is filled in, with the gap and labels across when the product has them. You can still change any value; the product name then shows Size changed from the product. Click Clear to stop using the product, or skip it and type the values yourself.

  • Width and Length of one label. Length is the size along the roll, in the direction the roll feeds through the printer.
  • Gap between labels along the roll, usually 2 to 3 mm (1/8 in). The ribbon moves past the gap too.
  • Labels across: labels side by side on the roll. The ribbon moves once for each row, so two labels across use half the ribbon.
  • Labels per roll and the Roll price.

When the open label is a roll product, it is chosen for you.

3. Ribbon

Enter the Length of one ribbon in meters or feet (for example 300 m or 1,476 ft), its Width and its Price. The width is only checked: you are warned when the ribbon is narrower than the labels across the roll.

Direct thermal printers print on heat-sensitive labels without a ribbon. Tick No ribbon (direct thermal) to leave the ribbon out.

4. Job

Enter the Labels to print you need, and a Waste percentage for setup, test prints and misprints. Waste labels use labels and ribbon like any other.

Results

Results update as you type. They are shown in five sections: Job, Labels, Ribbon, Cost (what this job uses) and Totals (what to buy).

ResultMeaning
RibbonsWhole ribbons to buy and their amount, the ribbon length needed and what is left on the last ribbon.
Label rollsWhole rolls to buy and their amount, and the labels left on the last roll.
Total to buyRibbons and rolls together.
Used by this jobOnly the labels and ribbon length this job uses. What is left over stays for the next job.
Cost per labelUsed by this job divided by the labels you need.

The ribbon needed is rows × (label length + gap), where rows is the labels printed divided by the labels across.

Use Cost per label to price a job: it counts only what the job uses, not the leftover ribbon and labels.

Saving the estimate

Click Save... and choose PDF or PNG for a page with the figures, or Text for plain figures you can paste into an email or quote.
