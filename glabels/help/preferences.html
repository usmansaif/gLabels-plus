Preferences and appearance

Open Edit > Preferences. Type a word such as grid, dark or mm in the search box to find a setting. Changes take effect immediately.

General

  • Startup folder: the folder shown first when you open or save a label: the folder gLabels Plus was started from, your home folder, your Documents folder, or another folder.
  • Recent files: clear the recent labels list.

Units

  • Measurement units: millimetres, centimetres, inches, points or picas.
  • Show unit names next to values in the Object properties panel.
  • Preferred paper sizes: ISO (A4...) or US (Letter...), used when designing your own products.

Editor

  • Show the grid, markup lines and rulers by default.
  • Grid spacing, and whether it starts at the top-left corner or the center of the label.
  • What objects align to: the selection, the last selected object, or the label.

Appearance

  • Color scheme: Light, Dark, or Same as my system.
  • Icon style: flat colorful icons or monochrome outline icons.

Window Layout

  • Page buttons (Edit, Properties, Merge, Variables, Print): icons and text, icons only or text only, and their size.
  • Toolbar buttons: icons only, or with text beside or below. Text helps while you learn the app.

Printing

Turn label outlines and crop marks on by default for every new print.

Keyboard shortcuts

Open Tools > Keyboard Shortcuts to change the shortcut of any menu command. Select a command, press the new keys, and click OK. Conflicts are shown so you don't assign one key twice. Reset restores one command and Reset All restores all of them. The Keyboard shortcuts page of this guide always shows your current shortcuts.

Reset everything

Use the reset button at the bottom of the Preferences window to return every preference to its original value.
