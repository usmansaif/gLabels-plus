Printing labels

Open the Print page with [[Ctrl+P]] or the Print button at the bottom left. The preview on the right shows exactly what will be printed.

Destination

Choose a printer, or Print to file (PDF) to save a PDF next to your label file. For advanced printer settings (paper tray, quality, duplex) click Use system print dialog....

What to print

Labels without merge data

  • Pages: print this many full sheets.
  • Positions ... to ...: print only some positions on one sheet, for example labels 7 to 15 on a partly used sheet.
Click a label in the preview to choose where printing starts; [[Shift]]+click to choose the last label. This is the quickest way to reuse a partly used sheet.

Labels with merge data

  • Copies: how many labels to print for each record.
  • Uncollated prints 1,1,1 2,2,2 3,3,3; Collated prints 1,2,3 1,2,3 1,2,3.
  • Merge groups are contiguous or start on a new page.
  • Start groups at position: the label position for the first record on the first sheet.

The line under the settings tells you how many items and pages will be printed.

Getting the alignment right

  1. In your printer settings, turn off Fit to page or Scale; print at 100%.
  2. Check that the printer's paper size matches the product (A4 or US Letter).
  3. Print one sheet on plain paper with outlines turned on and hold it against a label sheet up to the light.
  4. If everything is shifted the same amount, the printer feeds the paper slightly off. Many printer drivers have a margin or offset setting to correct this.

Thermal and roll printers

  • Install the manufacturer's driver and set its paper size to exactly the label size.
  • Choose a Barcode labels product of the same size in gLabels Plus.
  • Each label is sent as one page. If the printer skips labels or prints across the gap, calibrate the label sensor (see your printer's manual) and check the gap or black-mark setting in the driver.
  • Set the darkness or speed in the driver if barcodes come out faint or smudged.
