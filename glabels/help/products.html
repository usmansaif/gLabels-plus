Products, sheets and rolls

A product (also called a template) describes your label stock: the page size, the size and shape of each label, and how many labels there are on a sheet or roll.

Choosing a product

The product chooser opens when you create a new label. It lists over 1,800 products from Avery, Herma, Zweckform, Brother, Dymo, Uline, Worldlabel and many more.

  • Search by brand, part number, description or size, for example avery 5160, herma 4360, 2x4 or 70x37.
  • Filter by brand, category (address labels, business cards, CD/DVD, barcode labels...) and size.
  • Switch between grid and list views.
  • Click the star to add a product to your favorites. Products you have used recently are listed under Recently used.
  • Also sold as lists equivalent part numbers from other brands with the same layout.
Can't find your brand? Measure one label and search for its size. Many brands make sheets with the same layout as a popular Avery product.

Changing the product of an existing label

Click the page selector on the toolbar (next to Units), choose Edit > Page Setup ([[Ctrl+Shift+P]]) or click Change product on the Properties page. Objects keep their positions and sizes, so you may need to move them if the new label is smaller. The change can be undone in one step with [[Ctrl+Z]].

The Properties page

Press [[Ctrl+2]] to see the details of the current product:

  • Vendor, part number and description.
  • Page size, label size and layout (for example 3 × 10 labels).
  • Orientation: design the label horizontally or vertically. The label is rotated when printed so it still fits the stock.
  • Label length for continuous roll products, where you decide how long each label is.
  • Similar products that share this layout.

Thermal roll labels

The Barcode labels category contains 24 roll sizes for Zebra, TSC, Xprinter, Godex and similar printers, from 30 × 15 mm jewelry tags to 4 × 6″ shipping labels. Each roll label is printed as its own page, so set the same page size in your printer driver.

Products that are not listed

Create your own with Tools > Product Template Designer. See Designing your own product.

Markup lines

The dashed lines inside the label show the safe printing area and margins. Keep important text inside them, because printers cannot always print right up to the edge. Show or hide them with View > Markup.
