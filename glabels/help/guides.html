Smart guides, rulers and measuring

Tools that help you place objects exactly where they belong.

Smart guides and snapping

While you move or resize objects, red lines show the distance to the nearest object or label edge, in your chosen units.

  • Objects snap to other objects' edges and centers, and to the label's edges and center.
  • Hold [[Ctrl]] while dragging to turn snapping off for that move.
  • Equal spacing between objects is easy to see: the distance labels read the same.

Rulers

  • Rulers run along the top and left of the label and follow the zoom level.
  • Click the corner where the rulers meet to switch units (in, mm, cm, pt, pc).
  • Show or hide them with View > Rulers.

Grid

Turn on View > Grid to see a background grid. Set its spacing, and whether it starts at the top-left corner or the center of the label, in Preferences > Editor. Round, elliptical and CD labels always use the center.

Measure tool

  1. Press [[M]], click the ruler button on the toolbar, or choose Tools > Measure.
  2. Drag between any two points. The distance is shown in your units. Diagonal measurements also show the width and height.
  3. The ends snap to object and label edges. Hold [[Shift]] to keep the line horizontal or vertical.
  4. Press [[Esc]] to clear the measurement, or [[V]] to return to the Select tool.

Units

Choose inches, millimetres, centimetres, points or picas from the Units selector on the toolbar. The units apply to rulers, guides, the measure tool and the properties panel.

Live position tooltip

A small tooltip follows the cursor while you move, resize or draw, showing the current position and size.
