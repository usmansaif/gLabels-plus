Print sheet estimator

Before sending a job to a print shop, find out how many labels or pages fit on a large printing sheet, how many sheets you need and how much paper is wasted. Open it from Tools > Print Sheet Estimator.... It opens in its own window, so you can keep it open while you work.

1. Product

  • Label: one label of the product, printed and cut out on its own. This is the label you have open, or click Choose... to pick another product.
  • Page: the whole page of the product (for example a full A4 sheet of labels) printed as one piece.
  • Custom size: type any width and height.

Enter the Quantity you need. Choose millimeters, centimeters or inches at the top of the window.

2. Printing sheet

Pick a standard sheet from A4 up to B0, including press sizes such as SRA3, 12 × 18 in and 25 × 38 in, or choose Custom size. A sheet must be at least A4.

Margin is the edge on each side that the press cannot print on (the gripper). 5 mm is typical; ask your print shop for their exact value.

3. Bleed

Bleed is extra artwork around each item that is cut off after printing, so no white edge shows. The standard value is filled in for you: 2 mm (1/16 in) for labels and 3 mm (1/8 in) for pages and custom items. You can change it.

Results

Click Estimate Printing. The estimator tries upright and turned items, and mixes of both, and keeps the layout that fits the most items on a sheet.

ResultMeaning
Items per sheetHow many items fit on one sheet.
Sheets neededSheets to print the whole quantity.
WasteShare of each full sheet not used by finished items: margin, bleed and offcuts.
Job wasteThe same for the whole job, including unused places on the last sheet.

The preview shows the sheet: the shaded edge is the margin, light areas are bleed and strong areas are the finished items.

Try a few sheet sizes. A slightly larger or differently shaped sheet can fit noticeably more items.

Saving the estimate

Click Save... and choose PDF or PNG for a page with the figures and the sheet drawing, or Text for plain figures you can paste into an email or quote.
