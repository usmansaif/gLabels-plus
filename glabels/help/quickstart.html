Your first label in 5 minutes

This walk-through makes a simple product label with a name, a price and a barcode, then prints it.

1. Choose the label stock

  1. On the welcome screen click New label, or choose File > New ([[Ctrl+N]]).
  2. The product chooser opens. Type the brand and part number printed on your label packet, for example avery 5160, or a size such as 2x4 or 50x25.
  3. Select the product and click OK. An empty label of that size appears in the editor.
Using a thermal roll printer (Zebra, TSC, Xprinter, Godex)? Pick the Barcode labels category to see 24 common roll sizes.

2. Add text

  1. Press [[T]] (or click the Text button on the toolbar).
  2. Click on the label where the text should start, or drag to draw a text box of a set width.
  3. Type your text. Press [[Ctrl+Enter]] to finish, or click outside the text.
  4. With the text still selected, change the font, size and color in the Object properties panel on the right.

3. Add a barcode

  1. Press [[B]], then drag a rectangle on the label.
  2. Type the barcode data (for example ABC-12345) and press [[Enter]].
  3. Choose the barcode type (Code 128 is a safe default) in the Barcode tab of the properties panel.

4. Line things up

Drag objects into place. Red smart guides show the distance to the label edge and to other objects, and objects snap to edges and centers. To center something on the label, select it and choose Objects > Center > Center Horizontally.

5. Save

Press [[Ctrl+S]]. Labels are saved as .glabels files. If you work for several businesses, keep each one's labels in a project.

6. Print

  1. Press [[Ctrl+P]] or click the Print page button at the bottom left.
  2. Check the preview. On a sheet that is partly used, click the first empty label in the preview to start printing there.
  3. Set the number of copies or pages and click Print.
Print one test sheet on plain paper first and hold it against your label sheet to check the alignment.
