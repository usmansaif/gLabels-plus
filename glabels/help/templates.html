Designing your own product

If your labels aren't in the product list, describe them with the Product Template Designer (Tools > Product Template Designer).

Before you start

Measure a sheet of labels carefully with a ruler, in millimetres if you can. You will need:

  • The page size (for example A4 or US Letter), or the roll width.
  • The width and height of one label, and the corner radius if the corners are rounded.
  • The number of labels across and down.
  • The distance from the top-left corner of the page to the top-left corner of the first label.
  • The pitch: the distance from the left edge of one label to the left edge of the next one (and the same downwards).

Step by step

  1. Name: enter a brand and part number (for example Acme, AC-123) and a description.
  2. Page size: choose a standard paper size, or enter a custom size.
  3. Shape: rectangular (optionally with rounded corners), round, elliptical, or CD/DVD (including credit-card CDs).
  4. Size: the label dimensions for that shape, and a waste or margin value if needed.
  5. Layouts: one layout for a simple grid, or two layouts for sheets where alternate rows or columns are offset. Products that need more than two layouts must be edited by hand. Enter the number across and down (nx, ny), the distance from the left and top edges (x0, y0) and the horizontal and vertical pitch (dx, dy), then click Print test sheet and check it against a real sheet.
  6. Save: review the preview and click Save, or Back to change something.

Then make a new label with your product and print one sheet on plain paper. Hold it against a label sheet up to the light to check the fit.

Your product is saved in your personal templates folder and appears in the product chooser like any other product.

Continuous tape products are not supported by the designer yet. Use one of the built-in continuous or Barcode labels roll products instead.
