Glossary

TermMeaning
Product / templateA description of your label stock: page size, label shape and size, and how labels are laid out. See Products.
ObjectOne item on a label: text, barcode, image, box, line or ellipse.
LayoutA grid of identical labels on a sheet, described by the number across and down, the starting position and the pitch.
PitchThe distance from the edge of one label to the same edge of the next.
MarkupGuide lines inside the label showing the safe area and margins. They are never printed.
MergePrinting one label per record of a data file, such as a CSV file. See Mail merge.
RecordOne row of the merge file.
Substitution fieldA placeholder such as ${NAME} that is replaced with a value when printing. See Field syntax.
VariableA value you define, which can increase on every label. See Variables.
SymbologyA barcode type, such as Code 128 or EAN-13.
Quiet zoneThe empty margin a barcode needs on each side to scan.
Check digitAn extra digit calculated from the data, used by scanners to catch misreads.
Crop marksShort lines at the corners of each label showing where to cut.
CollatedPrinting copies as complete sets (1,2,3 1,2,3) rather than grouped (1,1 2,2 3,3).
ProjectA folder of related labels, for example for one business or client. See Projects.
GroupSeveral objects that move, align and lock together. See Layers and groups.
Smart guidesRed distance lines that appear while you drag objects. See Smart guides.
