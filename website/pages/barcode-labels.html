How do I use a barcode label preset?

  1. Open gLabels Plus and click New label... (or File > New).
  2. In the product chooser, select the Barcode labels category.
  3. Pick your size, for example 50 × 25 mm or 100 × 150 mm.
  4. Press B to add a barcode and T to add text, then print to your thermal printer.

Which size should I choose?

  • Retail price and MRP tags: 50 × 25 mm or 38 × 25 mm.
  • Jewelry and small parts: 30 × 15 mm.
  • Shelf, bin and product labels: 50 × 30 mm, 52 × 38 mm or 60 × 40 mm.
  • Courier and shipping labels: 100 × 150 mm or the exact 4 × 6″ (101.6 × 152.4 mm).
  • Pallets and warehouse locations: 100 × 100 mm or 101.6 × 101.6 mm.
