What is gLabels Plus?

gLabels Plus is a free, open-source label designer for Linux, Windows and macOS. It creates barcode, QR code, address, shipping and product labels, name badges, CD/DVD labels and business cards, prints them on sheet and thermal roll printers, and merges data from CSV files. Version {{version}} is maintained by Usman Saif and builds on gLabels by Jaye Evins.

Why switch to gLabels Plus?

A free alternative to paid and cloud label software, for shops, warehouses and anyone who prints a lot of labels.

  • Free, with no catch: no licence fee, trial, watermark or feature limit, for home or business use.
  • Works offline: design and print without an internet connection. Your labels and data stay on your computer.
  • No account: download, open and print. Nothing to sign in to.
  • Open source (GPL v3): the code is public, so the app can't be discontinued or locked behind a subscription.
  • Any printer: Zebra, TSC, Xprinter and Godex thermal printers, plus laser and inkjet sheet printers.
  • Windows, Linux and macOS: the same app and label files on every computer in your business.
