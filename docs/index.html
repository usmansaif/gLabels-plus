What is gLabels Plus?

gLabels Plus is a free, open-source label designer for Linux, Windows and macOS. It creates barcode, QR code, address, shipping and product labels, name badges, CD/DVD labels and business cards, prints them on sheet and thermal roll printers, and merges data from CSV files. Version 5.1.6 is maintained by Usman Saif and builds on gLabels by Jaye Evins.

New in 5.1.4Design with Claude

Describe the label. Claude builds it.

Open the Claude panel and say what you need in your own words. Claude picks the label stock, lays out text, barcodes, QR codes and symbols, checks a preview and fixes anything that doesn't fit. You watch every change happen in the editor, and Ctrl+Z undoes any step.

  1. Connect once. Tools > Connect AI > Claude links the Claude Code app on your computer, or the Claude Desktop app.
  2. Ask in the chat panel. Dock it on the left, right, top or bottom of the window, or show and hide it with Ctrl+Shift+K.
  3. Keep refining. "Make the order number bigger", "add our logo", "switch to 50 × 25 mm labels". Claude saves and exports a PDF proof when you ask.

Optional, and your choice. It uses Claude Code with your own Claude account; gLabels Plus has no AI service or subscription of its own. Your label goes to Claude only while you chat, and everything else keeps working offline. Included from version 5.1.4.

Why switch to gLabels Plus?

A free alternative to paid and cloud label software, for shops, warehouses and anyone who prints a lot of labels.

  • Free, with no catch: no licence fee, trial, watermark or feature limit, for home or business use.
  • Works offline: design and print without an internet connection. Your labels and data stay on your computer.
  • No account: download, open and print. Nothing to sign in to.
  • Open source (GPL v3): the code is public, so the app can't be discontinued or locked behind a subscription.
  • Any printer: Zebra, TSC, Xprinter and Godex thermal printers, plus laser and inkjet sheet printers.
  • Windows, Linux and macOS: the same app and label files on every computer in your business.

  • Social media logos and your own symbols: 31 social media and review logos (Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Google, Tripadvisor and more) join the symbols, now 151 in all. Add your own SVG files as symbols too; they stay on your computer and appear under My Symbols.

Frequently asked questions

What is gLabels Plus?

gLabels Plus is a free, open-source label and business card designer for Linux, Windows and macOS. It designs barcode, address, shipping and product labels, name badges, CD/DVD labels and business cards, with mail merge and over 1,800 ready-made templates. It is maintained by Usman Saif and built on gLabels by Jaye Evins.

Is gLabels Plus really free?

Yes. gLabels Plus is free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3 or later. There is no trial period, no watermark and no account, and you may use it for business labels.

Which operating systems does gLabels Plus support?

gLabels Plus runs on Linux (AppImage, .deb, .rpm, .tar.gz), Windows 10 and 11 (installer and portable .zip) and macOS (.dmg). It works fully offline.

Does gLabels Plus work with thermal label printers like Zebra, TSC or Xprinter?

Yes. If your operating system can print to the printer, gLabels Plus can. It includes 24 barcode label presets for thermal roll printers, from 30 × 15 mm to 4 × 6 inch shipping labels, and you can define your own roll sizes.

Is gLabels Plus a free alternative to BarTender, ZebraDesigner or Avery Design & Print?

Yes, for designing and printing barcode, product, address and shipping labels. gLabels Plus is free and open source, works offline on Windows, Linux and macOS, needs no account, and prints to any printer your computer can print to, including Zebra, TSC, Xprinter and Godex thermal printers. Avery sheet templates are built in, so you can design Avery labels without an internet connection. It prints through the normal printer driver and does not output printer languages such as ZPL.

Can I generate bulk barcodes from a CSV file?

Yes. Add a barcode object, set its data to a column from your CSV file, and gLabels Plus prints one label per row, each with its own barcode and text. Check the records in the live merge preview before printing, or print from scripts with glabels-batch-qt.
