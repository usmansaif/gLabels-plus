What's new in gLabels Plus 5.0

gLabels Plus 5.0, released on 22 November 2026, adds single-key tool shortcuts, quick font search, Projects for grouping labels by client, a page selector, 24 thermal barcode label sizes and a startup splash screen, on top of a redesigned editor, product chooser and Preferences.

Symbols for thermal labels

New

53 solid black symbols that print sharply on thermal printers: handling marks (Fragile, This Way Up, Keep Dry), food marks (Keep Refrigerated, Vegetarian, Gluten Free), contact icons (phone, email, website, location), delivery, shop, check boxes, stars and arrows.

HowTools > Insert Symbol (Ctrl+Shift+Y) · the window stays open while you add more

Curved text for round labels

New

Set text along an arc, round a circle, round a box or on a wave, or draw any circle, ellipse, box or line and put the text on it. Start angle turns the words round the curve a degree at a time, and distance from edge moves them in or out.

HowObjects > Put Text on Shape · Text tab > Path, Start angle, Distance from edge

Sticker labels for thermal printers

New

15 black and white sticker designs up to 4 in (101.6 mm) wide: round, oval, square and rectangle designs for cookies, bakery products, cottage food, jam, honey, candles, soap, coffee, cake boxes, spice jars, thank-you notes and box seals. Curved text around round labels, simple line drawings and clean type that prints sharply on a direct thermal printer.

HowFile > New from Sample > Stickers · replace the sample business name with your own

Sample label library

New

28 ready-made designs to start from: shipping label (4×6), sales receipt, A4 invoice, SKU, product, barcode, price tag, asset tag, address, bin location, carton/pallet, food date and QR code labels, plus 15 black and white stickers. Each one opens as an untitled copy, so the sample itself never changes.

HowFile > New from Sample or Start from a sample on the welcome screen · File > Save as Template adds your own

Smart guides and snapping

New

While you move or resize, red lines show the distance to the nearest object or label edge in your chosen units (mm, inches, points...). Objects snap to other objects' edges and centers and to the label's edges and center.

HowJust drag an object · hold Ctrl while dragging to turn snapping off

Measure tool

New

Measure anywhere on the label: between objects, to the label edge, or any two points. Shows the length, plus width × height for diagonal lines.

HowPress M or the ruler button · Shift keeps the line straight · Esc clears it

Layer groups and names

New

Name your objects and organize them into collapsible groups in the Objects panel. Select a group to move, align or lock all of it at once.

HowCtrl+G to group · double-click or F2 to rename · toolbar at the top of the Objects panel

Single-key tool shortcuts

New

Pick any drawing tool with one key, no menus needed.

  • VSelect
  • TText
  • RBox
  • LLine
  • OEllipse
  • IImage
  • BBarcode
  • MMeasure
  • EErase

HowHover a toolbar button to see its key · change them in Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts

Quick font search

New

Type part of a font name (mono, sans) in the Family box and pick from matches shown in their own typeface.

HowPress Enter or click a match to apply

Urdu labels in Nastaliq

New

Noto Nastaliq Urdu (regular and bold) is built in, so Urdu text is shaped and printed in true Nastaliq on every computer, even without the font installed.

HowType urdu in the Family box · Left, Center and Right align right-to-left text exactly as shown

Projects

New

Keep each business or client's labels together in one folder that's easy to back up or share.

HowFile > New Project · right-click to rename, duplicate or remove

Page selector

New

Change the page (product) at any time. Objects keep their positions and sizes, and one Undo reverts it.

HowToolbar, next to Units · Edit > Page Setup (Ctrl+Shift+P)

24 barcode label sizes

New

Thermal roll presets for Zebra, TSC, Xprinter and Godex, from 30 × 15 mm jewelry tags to 4 × 6″ shipping labels.

HowProduct chooser > Barcode labels category

Designing labels

  • Rulers along the top and left of the label. Click the units corner to switch units.
  • Objects panel lists every object from top-most to bottom-most. Click the lock column to lock an object so it can't be selected or moved.
  • Name objects and organize them into layer groups (Ctrl+G). A toolbar at the top of the Objects panel renames, locks, duplicates, groups, reorders and deletes the selection.
  • Smart guides show red distance lines to nearby objects and the label edge while you move or resize, and objects snap to edges and centers (hold Ctrl to turn snapping off).
  • Measure tool (M): drag between any two points to measure, in your chosen units.
  • Edit text in place: double-click a text or barcode object (or press Enter/F2) to type directly on the label.
  • Live position and size tooltip follows the cursor while you move, resize or draw objects.
  • Bring Forward / Send Backward moves objects one layer at a time.
  • Rotation box in the toolbar sets an exact angle for the selected objects.
  • Align to the whole selection, the last selected object, or the label itself.
  • Hold Shift with the arrow keys to nudge objects in larger steps.

Mail merge

  • Merge preview shows real records on the label instead of field names, with First / Previous / Next / Last buttons to step through them.

Products and templates

  • Sample label library: start from a ready-made shipping label, receipt, invoice, SKU, product, price, asset, address, bin, pallet, food date or QR code label, or one of 15 black and white stickers for bakery, cottage food, jar, candle, soap and coffee products. Opening a sample creates an untitled copy, so saving always asks for a new file name.
  • Save as Template keeps your own designs under My templates in the sample library.
  • Redesigned product chooser: search by brand, part number, description or size (for example avery 5160 or 2x4), with brand, category and size filters.
  • Grid and list views, plus favorites and recently used products.
  • Also sold as: see equivalent part numbers from other brands.
  • The product template designer uses your preferred units and supports roll labels.

Printing

  • Click a label in the print preview to choose where printing starts; Shift+click to choose the last label.
  • Default label outlines and crop marks can be set in Preferences.

Workspace

  • New welcome screen with quick actions and recent labels (right-click a label to remove it from the list).
  • Redesigned Preferences with a search box and sections for general, appearance, editor and printing options.
  • Light and dark color schemes and a choice of icon styles.
  • Custom keyboard shortcuts for every menu command (Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts).
  • Show or hide toolbars and panels, and choose icon size and text style for the page buttons and toolbars.
  • Units selector in the toolbar, and a zoom box that accepts typed values.
  • New About dialog with one-click copy of version information.
  • Refreshed application icon and a new set of toolbar icons.
