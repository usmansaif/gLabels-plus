Support gLabels Plus

gLabels Plus is free and open source, and stays free thanks to people who support it. You can donate, sponsor a feature your business needs, or contribute code, translations and label templates. Email the maintainer, Usman Saif, to donate.

How do I donate?

gLabels Plus is free and always will be. If it saves you time or money, you can support new features, more label templates and ready-to-run packages for every platform. Every contribution, big or small, is appreciated.

Email me and I'll share the donation options: bank transfer, PayPal, mobile wallets or sponsoring a feature your business needs.
